Find best rules

Searches the settings of the strategy for the best result under realistic conditions only: the band is rolling or expanding (past data only; tick “Change only within the current band” to keep the band you picked instead), every signal is filled at the next close, and your position mode, exposure and costs are kept. Each candidate is tuned on the first part of the period (train) and then run on the rest, which the search never looks at (test). By default a rule is scored by its weakest of three consecutive train segments, so it has to work consistently rather than in one lucky stretch. A rule that is good in train and poor in test is still overfitted: prefer rules that hold up in both.
